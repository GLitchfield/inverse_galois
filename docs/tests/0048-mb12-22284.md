## What the construction actually is

Not a radical over a degree-12 field (the deg-24 field has subfield-degree profile **[1,2,24]** —
its only proper subfield is a quadratic). It is a **Müller/Malle PGL(2,11) degree-12 Q(T)-family**
`gtil(T)` specialized at a **quadratic irrational** `theta = u + v·√d` over the real quadratic base
**Q(√d)**:

```
gtil(T) = (A⁴·(T+1249) − 364·B·C·D6)·T − (3³·5²·7·11/4)·Fq²·Gq        (A,B,C,D6,Fq,Gq fixed in Q[x])
q(y)   = y² − 2u·y + (u² − v²d)          (min poly of theta over Q)
E      = polredbest( rnfequation(q, gtil(y) mod q) )   → degree-24 absolute field
```

Two **independent dials**, both confirmed empirically here:

- **Signature r** = `prof(u+v√d) + prof(u−v√d)`, where `prof(t)` = #real roots of the degree-12
  specialization `gtil(t)`. Scanned profile: `prof(t)=2` for `t≲0`, `=12` for `t∈[1,14]`, `=0` for
  `t≳15`. So **both conjugates in [1,14] → r=24; one in [1,14] + one >15 → r=12; both <0 → r=4;
  both >15 → r=0.**
- **Group top (:C2/:V4/:C4/:D8 = 21362/22283/22284/23095)**: the **C4 = 22284** rung requires the
  norm `N(theta) = u²−v²d` to share `d`'s square class, i.e. **core(u²−v²d) = core(d)**. (V4=22283 has
  N a perfect square; the diagonal quadratic's entanglement with Q(√d) is what cyclicizes C4 vs V4.)

**Decisive Frobenius fingerprint** (the necessary local gate that isolates 22284 within the whole
cluster): cycle types **[24], [2,2,20], [8,8,8] PRESENT** and **[2,2,10,10], [2,22] ABSENT**. The
two absent classes rule out the Gassmann neighbour **21362** and the siblings **22283/23095** (which
carry [2,2,10,10]/[2,22] and lack [24]/[2,2,20]/[8,8,8]).

## Learnings

- The recovery's "base 12T297" / "degree-12 base" framing was a misnomer: the real base is the
  **real quadratic Q(√2)**, and 12T218/PGL(2,11) is the *Q(T)-family* gtil, not a subfield of the
  deg-24 field.
- **r and group are orthogonal dials**: r is set purely by where the two real conjugates of theta
  land in gtil's real-root profile; the group is set purely by `core(u²−v²d)`. This makes the whole
  `(t,r)` row of a cluster rung mechanically reachable from one family — the pattern generalizes to
  the siblings 21362 (:C2, core(N)=1·d…), 22283 (:V4, N a square), 23095 (:D8).
- Rebuilding through `polredbest` can *rescue scoreability* a raw recovered vector lacked (same
  field, smaller-index representative → faster nfdisc). Prefer rebuilding over submitting raw
  checkpoint vectors.
