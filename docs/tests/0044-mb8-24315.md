## Rarity of the 24315 correlation — measured, structural

The necessary norm condition `Res(c,g)·disc(c) ∈ (Q*)^2` (a specific ~10^8–10^12 integer must be a
perfect square) makes the correlation essentially unhittable by blind sweep:

- **0 correlated hits over ~8,470 general integer cubics** y^3+a2 y^2+a1 y+a0 (|a2|≤4, |a1|,|a0|≤14),
  fast norm pre-filter (checkpointed to `data/processed/mb8/normhits.txt`).
- **0 hits over ~17,000 points** on a 1-parameter reduction curve (ansatz β=u0+u1 y forcing the y/const
  coeffs; leaves `−14057/D(p)` = rational square, a positive-genus condition).

The underlying elliptic curve **E: v^2 = g(u)** has conductor 764016, trivial torsion, **analytic rank
1**, so an explicit parametrization is not rank-obstructed — but a single rational point on E gives a
FIXED PSL octic over Q (the diagonal [1,3,8,24] collapse), not the twist. Realizing 24315 requires the
character to equal the resolvent of a genuinely *varying* cubic ⇒ a real 2-descent / disc-correlation
construction, NOT a mechanical sweep or single-point substitution.
